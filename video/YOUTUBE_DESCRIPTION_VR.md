**Mitsinjo Grid VR — point at the world, build it in 3D, then power it.**

Put on a Meta Quest headset, look around a real 360° space, and turn any object you see into a 3D model: a fridge, a baroque sofa, a whole house, a car. Then drop it into a simulated rural solar microgrid village, walk around it freely, and control who gets electricity when the sun goes down.

Built solo for the Meta VR Hackathon.

🥽 **What you see**
• **Scan the room** in VR: the AI finds and outlines every object around you.
• **Pick one** and open the 3D studio: its 2D views (front, sides, back, top) appear around you. A view is off? Just ask for a change, by text or by voice.
• **Validate** and the 3D model is built, piece by piece, then opens in a 360° maker lab where you can turn it and inspect it.
• **Insert it into the village**, without leaving the headset: the same session switches to the microgrid simulation and the object is there, in the world.
• **Walk freely** through the village, use the map, fast-forward to the evening peak.
• **Control the power** from the dispatch controller's mixing desk: cut the homes and watch their lights fade, bring them back, and try to cut the health center. Refused: the clinic always keeps its power.

🤖 **Meta AI models in the app**
• **SAM 3.1** — outlines the detected objects
• **Muse Spark** — names objects, reasons about their parts, builds the 3D kit, guided by skill cards and specialist agents (facade specialist, critic)
• **Muse Image** — draws the 2D views of each object
• **Muse Voice** — speech to text for voice requests

⚡ **The energy side**
Every number on screen comes from a deterministic TypeScript engine (solar, battery, loads, dispatch strategies). The AI never invents energy values, and protected loads such as the clinic follow the village's rules. All energy data is simulated.
